ተልዕኮ / Mission

Carry out land preparation and transfer work that follows the procedure of the woreda and focuses on limited resources. By providing fair, efficient, and effective services through integrated management.

ራዕይ / Vision

To see an institution where a modern development and management system is established, excellent service is provided, and customer satisfaction is fully guaranteed.

እሴቶች / Values

  • Participation (ተሳትፎ)
  • Transparency (ግልጽነት)
  • Fairness (ፍትሃዊነት)
  • Superior Service (ላቀ አገልግሎት)
  • Accountability (ተጠያቂነት)

Welcome to Dalocha Woreda ወደ ዳሎቻ ወረዳ እንኳን በደህና መጡ

This service allows citizens to request vital event certificates electronically and enables authorized officers to review and approve applications.

ይህ አገልግሎት ዜጎች የወሳኝ ኩነት ምስክር ወረቀቶችን በኤሌክትሮኒክ መንገድ እንዲጠይቁ እና ሥልጣን ያላቸው ባለሙያዎች ጥያቄዎችን እንዲመረምሩና እንዲያጸድቁ ያስችላል።

Request and Approval Process የጥያቄና የማጽደቂያ ሂደት

1. Applicant 1. አመልካች

Applicant submits a request.

አመልካቹ ጥያቄ ያቀርባል።

2. Review 2. ግምገማ

Officer checks the application.

ባለሙያው ጥያቄውን ይመረምራል።

3. Decision 3. ውሳኔ

Officer approves or rejects.

ባለሙያው ያጸድቃል ወይም ውድቅ ያደርጋል።

4. Certificate 4. ምስክር ወረቀት

Approved certificate is issued.

የጸደቀው ምስክር ወረቀት ይሰጣል።
