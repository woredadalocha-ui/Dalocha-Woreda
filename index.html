ተልዕኮ / Mission

Carry out land preparation and transfer work that follows the procedure of the woreda and focuses on limited resources. By providing fair, efficient, and effective services through integrated management.

ራዕይ / Vision

To see an institution where a modern development and management system is established, excellent service is provided, and customer satisfaction is fully guaranteed.

እሴቶች / Values

  • Participation (ተሳትፎ)
  • Transparency (ግልጽነት)
  • Fairness (ፍትሃዊነት)
  • Superior Service (ላቀ አገልግሎት)
  • Accountability (ተጠያቂነት)
